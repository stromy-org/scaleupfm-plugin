**Version 2.0 · 2026-10-13 (ScaleUp refresh) · derived from `charter.json`**

### Essence

Senior CFO leadership that builds the finance function a scale-up needs: compliant, in control and optimised for growth.

### Positioning

ScaleUp Financial Management is the senior finance partner that takes a growing company from informal bookkeeping to a professional finance function, one stage at a time: op orde, in control, meer inzicht, beter schaalbaar. Diagnosis, roadmap, hands-on implementation and ongoing operation, led by Wilgert Hart with nearly 30 years of senior finance leadership and backed by a network of finance professionals.

### The four stages

The four bars of the Step Rail are four stages a client climbs. Services, site sections, deck chapters and the bridge imagery are ordered on this ladder.

| Bar | Stage (NL) | EN | Promise | Bridges |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Op orde | In order | Books that are right and compliant: bookkeeping, VAT, payroll and year-end on time, without surprises | small: canal bridges, wooden drawbridges, Magere Brug |
| 2 | In control | In control | A fixed month-end close and reporting you can steer on | medium: city cycle and footbridges |
| 3 | Meer inzicht | More insight | Forecasts, cash flow and KPIs that look ahead instead of back | large: river and harbour bridges |
| 4 | Beter schaalbaar | More scalable | Margins and working capital optimised, ready for investors, banks and acquisitions | biggest: the iconic spans |

### Audience

Owners and boards of NL-based start-ups making the scale-up transition (€1M–€20M revenue, growing fast, finance still informal).

### Personality

Senior · Calm · Structured · Pragmatic · Trustworthy

## 2. Name, tagline and headline

One name, one tagline, one headline.

| Element | Text | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | **ScaleUp** | Logo, short references in running text ("ScaleUp helpt…") |
| Full name | ScaleUp Financial Management | First mention, legal lines, signatures |
| Tagline | **Start to Scale** | Sign-off on every surface, NL and EN alike: email signature, closing slide, footer, social posts |
| Hero headline (NL) | Van risico naar regie. / Van intuïtie naar inzicht. / Van start-up naar serieuze scale-up. | Home page hero and deliverable covers |

**Rules:**
- Write the wordmark as one word with a capital S and U: ScaleUp. Never "Scale Up", "Scaleup" or "SCALE UP" in running text.
- "Start to Scale" is the tagline only; it is no longer the company name.
- Never translate the NL headline into English. EN surfaces lead with the tagline or an English headline written for them.
- Keep the rhythm of the NL headline: three lines, same meter, full stops.
- Retired: "Finance die met je meegroeit." and "Finance that scales with you." (replaced by the tagline).

## 3. Voice

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Plain Dutch, board-table register | Buzzwords ("synergy", "next-level", "agile") |
| Name what's broken, then say what fixes it | Vague reassurance |
| Use numbers when you have them | Use numbers as decoration |
| Short sentences. Then a longer one when nuance matters. | Long sentences that hide the verb |
| Treat the reader as a peer | Hype, urgency, "limited time" framing |
| Switch to English cleanly when the reader does | Mix NL and EN in the same sentence |

**Voice test:** Read it back as if Wilgert is delivering it across a boardroom table to a CEO he respects. If it sounds like a brochure, rewrite it.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logos/logo.svg` | Primary lockup on paper or white: navy rail, navy ScaleUp, amber descriptor. Default. |
| `logos/logo_white.svg` | Lockup on navy or on a photo: amber rail, white ScaleUp, amber descriptor. |

| `logos/logo_stacked.svg` / `logo_stacked_white.svg` | Rail centred above the wordmark, for square formats, covers and social avatars with room. |
| `logos/symbol.svg` / `symbol_white.svg` | Standalone Step Rail. Use when the name is already present. |
| `logos/icon_dark.svg` | Navy rounded tile with amber rail (app icon, raster source for every favicon PNG). |
| `logos/favicon.svg` | Theme-adaptive browser icon: lifted navy tile in dark browsers, navy tile in light browsers. |
| `logos/mask-icon.svg` | Single-colour rail for Safari pinned tabs. |
| `logos/favicon.ico`, `favicon-16/32/48/192/512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` | Raster icon set, all rendered from `icon_dark.svg`. |

Every PNG has a matching SVG and is rendered from it.

**Construction:**
- Step Rail: four bars in a fixed geometric ascent, each +18% taller; bar width 16 units, gap 6, 1px corner radius. One colour at a time.
- Wordmark: **ScaleUp** in Archivo ExtraBold (800), tracking −0.02em, a single colour. Never two-tone.
- Descriptor: `FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT` in Archivo SemiBold (600) caps, tracked so it spans exactly the width of the wordmark.
- Alignment: the top of the tallest bar sits on the cap height of ScaleUp; the foot of the rail sits on the descriptor baseline.
- All lettering is outlined paths, so the logo renders the same in every browser, PDF, Office file and print.

**Clear space:** at least the height of the capital S on every side.

**Minimum size:** full lockup 140px wide; short lockup 96px; symbol 16px (all four bars must stay visible).

**Don't:**
- Don't split the wordmark into two colours, and don't set "Scale" and "Up" as two words.
- Don't recolour bars individually, drop a bar or add a fifth.
- Don't round the bars beyond 1px, tilt or rotate the rail.
- Don't re-type the wordmark in a font; always use the supplied files.

## 5. Colour system

### Primary palette — Midnight Authority

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--brand-primary` (Midnight Navy) | `#0A1F3D` | Headlines, logo, primary surfaces, body text |
| `--brand-primary-alt` | `#1A3358` | Hover states, secondary surfaces |
| `--brand-accent` (Amber) | `#E8A33D` | CTA, signal, logo cap, single accents — never as body text |
| `--brand-background` (Paper) | `#FAF8F3` | Default background, warm off-white |
| `--brand-background-alt` | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, raised surfaces |
| `--brand-text` | `#0A1F3D` | Body text on paper |
| `--brand-text-light` | `#6B7280` | Captions, meta, secondary text |

### Semantic palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--success` | `#4A7A5A` | Positive states (forest, palette-aligned) |
| `--warning` | `#C28F3D` | Caution (amber-adjacent) |
| `--error`   | `#B85648` | Issues (muted brick) |
| `--info`    | `#4A6390` | Informational (navy-derived) |

Each semantic has a `-light` variant for background fills.

### Application rules

- **Accent is a signal**, not a decoration. One amber element per surface — the logo cap, a CTA button, a single data accent. Never both a button and an underlined link in amber on the same page.
- **Body text is navy** (`--brand-primary`), not pure black. Pure black reads cold and impersonal.
- **Backgrounds default to paper** (`#FAF8F3`), not pure white. Pure white reads as a software product; paper reads as a finance partner.
- **Dark sections** use navy (`--brand-primary`), with paper or white text and amber accent — for hero closes, key quotes, and section-of-honour treatment only.

## 6. Typography

| Role | Family | Weight | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / Headings | Archivo | 700–800 (Bold) | Google Fonts (OFL) |
| Body | Archivo | 400 (Regular) | Google Fonts (OFL) |
| Label / Eyebrow (Component L) | Archivo | 600, UPPER, tracked 0.20em | Google Fonts (OFL) |
| Mono / Data | JetBrains Mono | 400 | Google Fonts |

**Pairing logic:** Archivo is a confident, engineered grotesque with a wide weight range — it carries the Sage archetype as *diagnostic clarity* rather than literary warmth, and reads as modern and precise for a senior finance buyer. Hierarchy comes from weight + case + tracking within one family (Bold display → Regular body → 600-caps eyebrow), not a second typeface. JetBrains Mono is reserved for numbers, tables, and code-adjacent surfaces so columns align. The **eyebrow / small-title** is Archivo caps at 0.20em tracking — a deliberate brand signal, never the shared mono default.

**Type scale** (defined in `tokens.css`):

| Token | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--text-display-xl` | 56px | Hero on landing pages |
| `--text-display-lg` | 44px | Hero on internal pages |
| `--text-h1` | 32px | Page title |
| `--text-h2` | 26px | Section title |
| `--text-h3` | 22px | Subsection title |
| `--text-h4` | 18px | Card title |
| `--text-body-lg` | 17px | Lead paragraph |
| `--text-body` | 16px | Body |
| `--text-caption` | 12px | Captions, meta |
| `--text-overline` | 11px | Overlines (tracked 0.18em uppercase) |

**Rules:**
- Headings in Archivo Bold (700, or 800 for display) with `--tracking-tight` (-0.02em).
- Body in Archivo Regular with `--leading-body` (1.7).
- Overlines / eyebrows and the wordmark caption are tracked `--tracking-label` (0.20em), uppercase, Archivo Semibold (`.font-label` / `.overline`).
- Numbers in data contexts use JetBrains Mono so columns align.

## 7. Motif — Step Rail

**Construction:** Four rectangles in a fixed +18% geometric ascent (relative heights ≈ 0.40 / 0.59 / 0.79 / 1.00), bar width 16, gap 6, 1px corner radius. Single-colour: amber on navy, navy on paper. It doubles as the standalone symbol, the divider, and a data-bar language.

**Files:**

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `assets/svg/motif-step-rail.svg` | Primary motif — section openers, slide divider corners, hero accents |
| `assets/svg/divider-line.svg` | Section rule — short ascending rail at the left + navy rule |
| `assets/svg/divider-line-white.svg` | Same rule, white on dark |
| `assets/svg/pattern-tile.svg` | Background tile at 8–10% opacity — for slide backgrounds, never main reading surfaces |

**Where the motif lives:**
- Section openers in presentations (small, left-aligned over the section title)
- Top-right corner of business-card backs and PDF/slide covers
- Cover marks on PDF reports
- The left of horizontal dividers (the ascent encodes the "start → scale" direction)
- As a hero accent beside the NL three-line headline
- Micro-scale: KPI-row bullets and data annotations (the rail reads as a mini bar-language)

**Don't:**
- Don't use the motif more than once as a *signature* per visible surface (its data-bar use is separate).
- Don't drop a bar or add a fifth. Four bars, +18% ascent, is the meaning.
- Don't round the bars beyond 1px or tilt/rotate the rail. It is upright, always.

## 8. Imagery direction

**One subject: Dutch bridges, from small to big.** Each of the four stages has its bridge register, so a deck or page moves from canal bridges to the iconic spans as the story climbs. Only bridges in the Netherlands, and never a photograph that appears in another client's library (the same bridge from a different shot is fine).

| Register | What it shows | Typical roles |
|---|---|---|
| Hero / cover | A clear bridge subject with a calm area of sky or water for a headline | cover, hero |
| Background | Quiet, uniform bridge details: deck planks, steps, railing rhythm, brick arches, cable fans, water under a span | background, texture |
| Divider | A strong horizontal line: a long low span, a deck edge on the horizon | divider |
| Closing | The biggest spans, calm and conclusive | closing |

**House look (treatments, applied at render time, never baked into the library):**
- `daylight` (default): natural colour, slightly muted, warm paper lift in the highlights.
- `navy-duotone`: navy shadows to paper highlights, for covers where type sits on the photo.
- `amber-graded`: muted with a touch of amber, for a warm section moment.
- `paper-mono`: black-and-white with fine grain, for background textures only.

The final house look is confirmed by Wilgert on the imagery board.

**Rules:**
- Calm daylight, overcast or blue hour; level horizons; no dramatic high-contrast black and white as the house style.
- Text on an image sits on a navy scrim in the text-safe zone recorded per image; the logo on an image is always the white variant.
- Every library image carries `theme` (stage), `roles`, `geometry.focalPoint` and a credit with its licence.
- Avoid: bridges outside the Netherlands, mountains or generic Dutch landscape, handshakes and suits in meetings, people as the subject.

## 9. Component patterns

Tokenised utility classes ship in `tokens.css`:

- `.btn-primary` — navy button on paper, body text in paper colour. Default CTA.
- `.btn-accent` — amber button on paper, body text in navy. Used once per page max — the "talk to Wilgert" close.
- `.btn-ghost` — outlined navy button on paper. Secondary actions.
- `.card` — white card on paper with thin neutral border.
- `.badge-success / -warning / -error / -info` — small semantic badges.
- `.divider` — horizontal rule with the navy-90 / amber-10 split.
- `.overline` / `.font-label` — 11px Archivo Semibold uppercase, tracked 0.20em (Step Rail eyebrow).
